## 🌿 Why Sundarban?

The Sundarbans is the **world's largest mangrove forest** (~10,000 km², ~6,000 km² in Bangladesh) and a **UNESCO World Heritage Site** since 1997. It is the last stronghold of the **Royal Bengal Tiger**.

**Realistic wildlife expectations:**
| Animal | Sightings Chance |
|---|---|
| Spotted deer (chital) | 🟢 Near certain |
| Monkeys (rhesus macaque) | 🟢 Near certain |
| Birds (~300 species) | 🟢 Constant |
| Estuarine crocodile | 🟡 Likely (esp. Karamjal) |
| Wild boar | 🟡 Moderate |
| Royal Bengal Tiger | 🔴 Rare — treat as bonus |

> ⚠️ Any operator promising a guaranteed tiger is lying. Plan around the forest, not the tiger.

## 📅 3-Day Itinerary

Standard Khulna → Sundarban → Khulna route (tide-dependent):

### Day 1 — Khulna → Harbaria → Kotka
- 06:00 Board at Rupsha Ghat
- Permit + armed guard at Dhangmari/Chandpai station
- Harbaria Forest Office visit
- Cruise to Kotka, watchtower sunset
- Overnight on ship

### Day 2 — Kotka → Jamtala → Kochikhali
- Dawn dinghy canal cruise (birdwatching)
- Mangrove walk + Jamtala sea beach
- Kochikhali Forest Office (deer, monkeys)
- **BBQ dinner** (trip highlight)
- Overnight at Dhangmari/Chandpai

### Day 3 — Karamjal → Khulna
- Karamjal Eco-Park + crocodile breeding center
- Return cruise, permit surrender
- Arrive Khulna by evening

## 🛡️ Safety & Forest Rules

- **Never** step off the ship into the forest without an armed guard — illegal and dangerous
- Stay on marked boardwalks at Karamjal / Kotka / Katka
- No plastic (fine enforced), no feeding wildlife
- No alcohol/smoking on most vessels
- Don't lean over railings; don't walk the deck alone at night
- Itinerary may shift due to tide/weather/Forest Department orders

## 📞 Booking Tips

1. **Book 2 weeks ahead** for weekends; 1 month for Dec–Jan peak
2. **Ask for cabin layout + food menu** before paying — good operators publish both
3. **50% advance** is standard (bKash/Nagad/bank)
4. **Submit NID copies** early — forest permit needs them
5. Prefer **ATAB-registered** operators with real corporate references
